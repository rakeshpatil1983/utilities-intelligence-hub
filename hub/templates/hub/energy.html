Reading the transformer findings…

Health index: 100 minus each finding's weight. Forecast: weekday × 15-minute profile of the last 4 weeks; P(overload) from the 8-week back-test spread. Five-month outlook: each transformer's own peak on the same dates last year × its year-on-year growth. Ratings are inferred from each unit's heaviest-phase current where the nameplate is not in the data.
